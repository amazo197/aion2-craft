Methodology

Recipes and icons come from the gamers4.life crafting graph. Prices are the cheapest current listing on aion2.exchange for the chosen server, refreshed hourly by a GitHub Action. 28-day sales statistics exist only where the game publishes them (materials, consumables, skins), so for gear and accessories the "cleared band" is inferred from listings that disappeared between 10-minute snapshots: the low end is the cheapest ask that vanished, the high end the next ask left standing. Disappearances include cancels and relists.

cost

Ask cap. The cheapest ask is only used as a sale price when it sits within the cap (default 20%) of the highest known trade. The highest known trade is the 28-day maximum sale where the game publishes one, otherwise the highest ask that has ever vanished from the book, carried forward across bakes. Above the cap the item is valued at that trade instead, and the row is marked capped.

Success rate is yours to set per mastery tier; Korean testing found ~95% on beginner recipes and ~65% on Dragon-King-tier recipes at matching mastery. A failed craft loses all materials. Bound NPC consumables (abrasive, catalyst, hardener, bottles) are costed at the NPC price input. Inputs bought from the market are priced at the higher of the cheapest ask and the cheapest ask that vanished in the window, so a lone low listing does not set the cost.
